The 4% rule, established by financial researcher William Bengen in 1994, states that if you withdraw 4% of your retirement corpus in the first year and increase that amount by inflation each subsequent year, your money has a very high probability of lasting 30+ years. This calculator uses this rule as a default but lets you test any withdrawal rate from 1% to 12%.

We use real CAGR data from 14+ Indian mutual funds and indices sourced from AMFI, apply actual Indian tax rules (12.5% LTCG on equity above ₹1.25L, 30% on debt), and factor in expense ratios. The calculator models inflation-escalated withdrawals year by year, not simplified averages.

SWP is the opposite of SIP. Instead of investing a fixed amount monthly, you withdraw a fixed amount from your mutual fund corpus each month. This calculator simulates SWP across your entire retirement period, increasing withdrawals with inflation each year. It shows whether your corpus survives or gets depleted, and at what year.
